1 Who we are

The ANU CDE Student App is the official mobile application of the Centre for Distance Education, Acharya Nagarjuna University, Nagarjuna Nagar – 522510, Guntur District, Andhra Pradesh, India.

The University is the data fiduciary for the information described in this policy. The app is provided only to students enrolled in a distance-education programme of the Centre. It is not open to the general public, and there is no self sign-up — your account exists because you are already enrolled with us.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else sees it, and what you can ask us to do about it. If anything here is unclear, please contact the Grievance Officer listed in section 13.

2.2 What you give us through the app

  • Your password. On your first sign-in you use your date of birth, then you choose a password. It is stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash — nobody at the Centre can read it.
  • Grievances. The category, subject, description and every message you send in a grievance thread.
  • Help Desk questions. The questions you type to the in-app assistant. See section 5 — these are processed by a third party.

2.3 Collected automatically

  • Sign-in records — the date, time and IP address of each sign-in, kept as a security measure.
  • Session token — a temporary token stored on your device to keep you signed in. It expires after 24 hours and is deleted when you sign out.
What we do not collect No location or GPS data. No contacts, call logs or SMS. No advertising identifier. No third-party analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising, and we do not build behavioural profiles of students.

5 The Help Desk chatbot

The app includes a Help Desk assistant that answers questions about the app, your fee status and the Centre. It is powered by an artificial intelligence service operated by Groq, Inc., a company based in the United States.

Please read this before using the Help Desk When you send a message, your question is transmitted to Groq’s servers outside India, together with your name, enrollment number, course, batch, learner support centre and total fee due. This context is what lets the assistant answer questions such as “how much fee do I still owe?”
  • Do not type sensitive information into the Help Desk. Never enter your password, your full Aadhaar number, or card or bank account details.
  • Your chat is not stored on our servers. It is held only in your device’s memory for the duration of the conversation and is cleared when you close the app.
  • Groq processes the message to generate a reply and handles it under its own privacy terms. We do not control Groq’s retention practices.
  • The assistant cannot change any university record, make a payment, or view your examination results.
  • Using the Help Desk is entirely optional. Every feature of the app works without it. If you would rather not have your data sent abroad, simply do not open the Help Desk, and call or email the Centre instead.

Answers from the assistant are generated automatically and may be incomplete or wrong. For anything that affects your fees, examinations or certificates, rely on the official figures in the app or contact the Centre directly.

7 Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and University policy, you may:

  • See your information. The Profile tab in the app already shows you everything we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it. Open Profile → Request Correction in the app, which files a grievance with the Records Section. You may be asked for a supporting certificate.
  • Ask us to erase it, subject to the statutory limits in section 8.
  • Complain. Raise a grievance in the app, or contact the Grievance Officer in section 13. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Decline the Help Desk. Not using it stops any transfer of your data outside India.

We will acknowledge a request within seven working days and respond within thirty days. There is no fee.

8 Deleting your data

You can ask us to remove your data from the app. Please be clear about what can and cannot be deleted.

What we will delete on request

  • Your app password, so the account can no longer be signed into
  • Your grievances and their message threads
  • Your sign-in history
  • Your profile photograph

What we cannot delete

Your academic record, admission record and fee and payment history are the University’s official records. We are legally required to keep them so that your degree, marks and dues can be verified in future, and so the University can meet its audit and statutory reporting obligations. Deleting your app account does not remove them, and they would remain even if the app did not exist.

How to request deletion

Email director_cde@anu.ac.in from the email address in your student record, with the subject line “App data deletion request”, stating your name and enrollment number. You may also raise a grievance in the app under the category Other.

We will confirm within seven working days and complete the deletion within thirty days.

9 How we protect your information

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes and are never logged or emailed.
  • Your session token expires after 24 hours and every request is checked against it, so one student cannot open another student’s records.
  • Only your own department’s study material, your own fee record and your own grievances are returned to your device.
  • Staff access to the administrative side of the app is restricted, and changes to a grievance are recorded against the member of staff who made them.
  • Location data is stripped from any photograph uploaded by staff.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. If you believe your account has been accessed by someone else, change your password in the app and tell us at once.

11 Children

The app is intended for students enrolled in distance-education programmes of the Centre, who are ordinarily eighteen years of age or older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under eighteen except where such a person has been lawfully admitted to a programme of the University.

Where an enrolled student is under eighteen, their data is processed only for the educational purposes described in this policy, we do not use it for tracking or behavioural advertising of any kind, and consent is obtained from a parent or lawful guardian at the time of admission as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or when the law requires it. The date at the top of this page always shows the current version.

If a change materially affects how your information is used — for example a new third-party service, or a new purpose — we will tell you through a notice in the app before the change takes effect. Please review this page from time to time.
